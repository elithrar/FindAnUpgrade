# FindAnUpgrade 0.1.13

Stars use one native empty/filled texture at 15% smaller visual size, with no
extra outline on the filled state. Rewards sit below the complete star button,
and Guaranteed/Choose one captions have measured space around them.

Options keeps all settings in one window with five sections and no tabs.
Short screens scroll while title, close and Reset stay fixed. Quests and
Shortlist keep their tabs, independent queries and scroll positions.

The settings schema, saved shortlist and item icon sizes are unchanged.
Screenshot inspection and offline constructor checks support the layout fix;
actual patched-client rendering, physical input, item/comparison transit and
combat/taint remain pending live checks. No CurseForge publication.
